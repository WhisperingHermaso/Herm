Herm

One small hermit crab. One hungry beach. Far too many gulls.

  • HoldKeep a finger on the screen and Herm walks toward it. Eat what the waves wash up.
  • Let goLift your finger and Herm hides in the shell. Do it when a gull's shadow lands on you.
  • GrowFood makes Herm bigger. Once the shell is too tight, hiding stops working, so walk over a bigger shell to move in.

On a computer: hold ← → (or A / D) to walk, let go to hide. P pauses.
